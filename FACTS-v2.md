## Conflicts in the slides (flagged, not stated as numbers)
- **Worktop thickness:** slide 1 says 30 mm and slide 4 says 25 mm. The film uses **30 mm**.
- **Lower shelf size:** the text says "L350 × W240 × T30 mm" and the CAD callout says "350 × 2440 T30mm". The side sketch shows the shelf about 385 mm deep, about 321 mm above the floor. The film models one full-length rear shelf about 400 mm deep and states only "30 mm".
- **Outlets:** the spec text says 4 outlets. A sketch note says "Universal 220V 8 plug" with a 3 m cord. The film follows the text: **4 outlets**.
- **Cart height:** 900 mm is shown as the body height. The push handle rises above it, as in the reference photo.
- **Lamp:** "8M AC wire" is not shown; the film only states 8×.

## Modelling choices not in the slides (never labelled or spoken as numbers)
- Cabinet mounted 600 mm above the worktop (from the side sketch), shelf height, light housing depth, lamp arm lengths, chair seat height, the outlet face style, bin and notebook sizes, the tape footprint, and the boxes on the cart. All of these are illustrative.
- The tools are simplified shapes, not the exact Mitutoyo / Mitsuwa models.
- The cabinet door is drawn top-hinged (the slides don't give the hinge side). A soft light inside the open cabinet keeps the tools readable.
- ESD claims are made only where the slides make them: the worktop and shelf laminate, the lamp, the bin and the chairs. The frame is not called ESD.
- Operators are translucent placeholder figures, not real people.
