## Modelling choices not in the spec (never labelled or spoken as numbers)
- Worktop height 850 mm; cabinet mounted 1450–1850 mm on the rear uprights; light housing 46 mm deep; lamp arm lengths; chair seat height; outlet face style; tape footprint size; the cabinet contents and the boxes on the cart. All illustrative.
- The cart's push handle rises above its 600 mm body, as in the reference photo; the 600 mm label refers to the body.
- The cabinet door is drawn top-hinged (one full-width swing door). The spec doesn't give the hinge side.
- The ESD claims are only made for the lamp and the chairs, as in the spec. The worktop and frame are not called ESD.
- Operators are translucent placeholder figures, not real people.
